About PMRC

Welcome to the Catholic Precision Medicine Research Center

The Catholic Precision Medicine Research Center (PMRC) is dedicated to advancing translational medicine, bridging the gap between basic research and clinical practice. We lead patient-centered precision medicine research through artificial intelligence and state-of-the-art genomic analysis technologies.

Our goal is to pioneer a new paradigm in precision medicine by leveraging AI technology and to realize data-driven personalized treatments.

  • Developing and advancing precision medicine analysis technologies
  • Providing precision medicine education for medical professionals and researchers
  • Supporting genomic data production and AI-based analysis
  • Creating innovative models through industry-academia collaboration

Key Activities

Our Activities

Current research in detail

01

Advanced Genomics Research

We are leading academic advancements in the precision medicine field by conducting national research projects and publishing papers in prestigious SCI-indexed international journals.

02

AI-Based Precision Medicine

We utilize artificial intelligence and machine learning to analyze vast genomic data and develop predictive models for early disease detection.

03

Professional Education Programs

We offer hands-on training and workshops for medical professionals and researchers on topics such as genomic big data analysis and Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS).

04

Global Network

We are building a collaborative network with world-class researchers by co-hosting domestic and international academic conferences and organizing symposiums.

05

Technology Development and Commercialization

We develop technologies such as rapid on-site infection tests and AI diagnostic algorithms, pursuing commercialization through patent applications to translate research outcomes into practical clinical applications.

Research support services

Cancer Gene Panel Analysis
Genetic mutation testing for personalized cancer treatment.
Whole Exome Sequencing (WES)
Analyzing all protein-coding genes to identify the causes of rare diseases.
Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS)
Decoding an individual's entire genetic makeup to provide comprehensive health information.
Long-Read Sequencing
Precisely analyzing complex genetic variations that are difficult to detect with conventional methods.
Single-Cell Analysis
Investigating disease mechanisms by analyzing gene expression at the individual cell level.
AI Data Analysis
Interpreting complex genomic data and deriving clinical insights using artificial intelligence.
