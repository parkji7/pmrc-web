Services

Main Services

Main analysis services of the Catholic Precision Medicine Research Center
Service Description
Single-Cell Genomic Analysis10X Genomics Genomic analysis at single-cell resolution to characterise cellular heterogeneity, identify rare cell populations and trace cell differentiation trajectories.
Whole Transcriptome AnalysisIllumina Whole transcriptome sequencing to profile gene expression and detect transcript-level variation, including alternative splicing.
Whole Exome AnalysisIllumina Sequencing of the entire exome to analyse and diagnose disease-associated genetic variants.
Droplet Digital PCRBio-Rad QX200 system Absolute quantification of trace amounts of DNA/RNA for the precise detection of rare variants and copy number variation (CNV).
OncoChase Cancer Panel SequencingIon S5 / Genexus system Targeted sequencing of 95 key cancer-related genes to support precise diagnosis and personalised treatment decisions.
OncoChase Cancer Panel SequencingIllumina An expanded panel of 157 cancer-related genes for comprehensive cancer diagnosis and treatment strategy.
