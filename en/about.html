About the Center

Since its establishment in 2016, the Catholic Precision Medicine Research Center has achieved continuous growth through the research activities of its medical staff and research team.

Director’s Message

The Catholic Precision Medicine Research Center was established in 2017 after preparation beginning in 2015, with the vision of becoming a hub for precision medicine—the future of healthcare. During this time, we have built a solid foundation for the practical application of precision medicine through research utilizing cancer gene panels and other initiatives.

Currently, the Center is actively conducting various cutting-edge research projects, including the development of genomic analysis technologies for cancer and complex diseases, disease gene analysis panel development, patient-derived organoid research, and the development of molecular markers crucial for personalized treatment selection and disease prognosis prediction. Additionally, we are working to accelerate the practical implementation of precision medicine through analytical technology support for clinical applications, discovery of novel drug candidates, and training of precision medicine specialists.

Moving forward, we will continue to pursue our goals of strengthening education, expanding research support, and enhancing international collaboration in the field of precision medicine. With the inclusion of NGS-based cancer panel testing in national health insurance coverage, we plan to further accelerate the development and dissemination of Korean cancer panels. Furthermore, we intend to leverage the extensive network of the Catholic Medical Center to establish a precision medicine database and effectively integrate it into cancer treatment technology development and clinical practice. Throughout this process, we will dedicate significant efforts to the discovery and validation of novel molecular markers.

Ultimately, our Center’s goal is to provide optimized healthcare services tailored to each individual patient. We look forward to opening new horizons in precision medicine through your continued interest and support.

Thank you.

Yeon-Jun Chung, M.D., Ph.D.Director, Catholic Precision Medicine Research Center

Organization

Organizational Structure

The Catholic Precision Medicine Research Center has established a systematic organizational structure to advance cutting-edge precision medicine research and development. Led by the Center Director, the Operating Committee and Technical Advisory Department provide strategic direction and expert guidance for the Center. Building on this foundation, three core departments work collaboratively to shape the future of precision medicine.
